# GPT 壁纸提示词模板（9:16 竖屏）

## 模板

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper of <ROLE> from <GAME>, the <KEYWORD1>, 
<KEYWORD2>, <LOOK_DETAILS>, <OCCUPATION/WEAPON>, <SCENE>, 
official game art style, no text.
```

## 已实测示例

### Hu Tao（胡桃）— ✅

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper of Hu Tao from Genshin Impact, the cheerful girl with brown twintails, red eyes, black and red outfit, holding her Staff of Homa, plum blossoms and butterflies, night shrine background, official game art style, no text.
```

### Raiden Shogun（雷电将军）— ✅

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper of Raiden Shogun from Genshin Impact, the elegant woman with long purple hair in a side braid, calm violet eyes, purple and white kimono, holding her Musou no Hitotachi, standing under a dramatic purple storm sky with lightning, falling sakura petals, official game art style, no text.
```

### Ganyu（甘雨）— ✅

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper of Ganyu from Genshin Impact, the graceful woman with long blue-violet hair and small curved horns, calm aqua eyes, white and blue elegant qipao-style dress, holding her ornate bow, standing on a snowy mountain cliff with snowflakes falling, soft icy blue glow, official game art style, no text.
```

## 关键规则

1. 必须含 `official game art style, no text`（官方风格 + 无文字水印）
2. 必须含 `vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper`
3. 角色特征写 3-4 个（发型/瞳色/服饰/标志物），场景 1-2 个
4. 用 `from Genshin Impact` 等来源声明（点名成功率高）
5. 避免 `girl`（若被拦）：改用 `woman / lady / elegant figure`

## 输出

941×1672（9:16）JPG → 下载为 `raw/<role>_wall_raw.jpg` → 4K 转 `*_wall_4k.jpg`
